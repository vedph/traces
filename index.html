Traces

Philology and Forensic Methods in Born-Digital Literary Archives

Venice, 26-27 November 2026

Traces is a conference of BLADE (opens in a new tab), a research project of Ca’ Foscari University of Venice on the born-digital archives of writers: floppy disks, hard drives, word-processing files. The conference is held in English, in Venice and on Zoom.

When
Thursday 26 November, 14:30-18:30
Friday 27 November, 9:30-13:15
Where
Sala polifunzionale Maria Rimoldi, Cannaregio 980, Calle della Madonna, Venice (map (opens in a new tab))
Online
All sessions can be followed on Zoom: register to receive the link (opens in a new tab). The sessions will not be recorded.
