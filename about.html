The conference

Traces. Philology and Forensic Methods in Born-Digital Literary Archives is a conference of the BLADE project. It takes place in Venice on 26 and 27 November 2026, in the Sala polifunzionale Maria Rimoldi (Cannaregio 980), and can be followed on Zoom. The programme has a keynote lecture by Thorsten Ries, two sessions of papers on writers’ born-digital archives, a presentation of the update of the Digital Preservation Coalition’s Technology Watch Report on digital forensics, and three hands-on workshops.

The BLADE project

BLADE (Born-digital Literary Archives DNA Exploration) studies the preservation, analysis and interpretation of born-digital literary archives. The archives of late twentieth-century writers increasingly contain digital media: floppy disks, hard drives, word-processing files, email. These materials keep traces no manuscript has: overwritten versions, deleted fragments, system dates, residues of the programs in which the text was composed. BLADE asks how these traces can become philological evidence, and adapts the procedures of digital forensics to philological inquiry.

BLADE is a SPIN 2025 project of Ca’ Foscari University of Venice, hosted by the Department of Humanities and the Venice Centre for Digital and Public Humanities (VeDPH). It runs from 1 June 2026 to 31 August 2027. Principal investigator: Emmanuela Carbé.
